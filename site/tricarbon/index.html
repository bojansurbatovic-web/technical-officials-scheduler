Sustainability

TriCarbon

For iPhone, and for Ubuntu from the Snap Store

Estimate the carbon footprint of a triathlon or multisport event: per competitor, spectator, staff member and volunteer, and for the event as a whole. An ISO 14001 environmental aspects register sits alongside it.

Screenshots

A look inside TriCarbon.

Real screens from the iPhone app, showing its sample event. Scroll sideways to see them all.

What it does
  • Around 110 built-in emission factors for transport, accommodation, food, energy, waste and equipment
  • Totals, per-person figures, charts by category and suggestions for reductions
  • A travel estimator that turns a short survey into inventory lines
  • An ISO 14001 environmental aspects register, pre-filled for triathlon
  • PDF and Excel reports in English or Montenegrin
  • The built-in factors are indicative: for audited reporting, replace them with official national factors

Technical Officials Scheduler

For iPhone, iPad, Mac, the web and Ubuntu

Plan the Technical Officials of a triathlon event in minutes: every official gets a main position, short jobs are added as second positions without time clashes, and the meetings and briefings the rules require (Elite athletes’ briefing at 16:00 the day before, Jury meetings, rehearsals, TO briefings) are booked for the right people.

See the plan as a table, personal schedules, a Gantt chart and a timeline, and export it to Excel, LibreOffice, your calendar or a picture.

Open the web versionSource code

What it plans

  • Races of any World Triathlon format: super-sprint, sprint, standard, mixed relay, middle and long distance – over one or several days.
  • Positions and duty windows from the World Triathlon TO Checklist 2025; rules from the Competition Rules; race-week schedule from the Event Organisers’ Manual.
  • Who works which race, availability, preferred positions, locked choices, and radios needed.
  • Your events stay on your device. No account, no tracking.

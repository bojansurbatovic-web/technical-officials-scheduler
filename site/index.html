Sustainability

TriCarbon

Estimate the carbon footprint of a triathlon or multisport event: per competitor, spectator, staff member and volunteer, and for the event as a whole.

  • Around 110 built-in emission factors for transport, accommodation, food, energy, waste and equipment
  • Totals, per-person figures, charts by category and suggestions for reductions
  • An ISO 14001 environmental aspects register, pre-filled for triathlon
  • PDF and Excel reports in English or Montenegrin

Training

TriAnatomy

A strength-training companion for triathletes. See which muscles each movement works, then build and run workouts and training plans for the swim, the bike and the run.

  • 132 strength exercises, plyometric drills and stretches with step-by-step instructions
  • An animated body model where the working muscles light up
  • Workout builder with heart-rate zones and a guided timer
  • Periodised plans for your calendar and Garmin .fit export

Officiating

TriOfficial

A reference tool for triathlon technical officials. Pick your role and your race, and see the duties before, during and after the event, the key race figures, and the rules and penalties that apply.

  • Checklists for Technical Delegates, referees, chief race officials and more
  • Triathlon, duathlon, aquathlon, cross, winter and aquabike, from super sprint to long distance
  • Tick off duties, add notes and export a report
  • Educational only: the official Competition Rules always take precedence

Race-day planning

Technical Officials Scheduler

Plan the technical officials of a triathlon event in minutes. Every official gets a main position, short jobs are added without time clashes, and the required meetings and briefings are booked for the right people.

  • Any race format, from super-sprint to long distance, over one or several days
  • Table, personal schedules, Gantt chart and timeline views
  • Export to Excel, LibreOffice, calendar or a picture
  • No account, no tracking: events stay on your device

    Why these apps exist

    What technical officials do.

    Technical officials help every athlete race on a safe and fair field of play. This is a general, informal overview of the work the apps are meant to support.

    Field of play safety

    Checking the swim start and course, the bike and run routes, and access for medical teams before and during the race.

    Applying the rules

    Observing equipment, uniforms and conduct on course, and applying the competition rules consistently for everyone.

    Transition and penalties

    Watching mount and dismount lines, racking and helmet use, and managing time penalties where they apply.

    Results and reviews

    Supporting athlete briefings, handling protests and appeals through the proper process, and confirming results.

Privacy policy

Technical Officials Scheduler · last updated 11 November 2026

Summary

The app does not collect any data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not send anything over the internet.

What stays on your device

The events you create – including the names of officials you type in – are saved only on your device: as files in the app’s own folder on iPhone, iPad and Mac (visible in the Files app on iPhone and iPad), or in your browser’s storage when you use the web version. Deleting the app deletes them.

Sharing and exports

When you export or share a plan (Excel, LibreOffice, calendar, picture or plan file), the file goes only where you send it, using your device’s share sheet or save dialog. What happens to it there is governed by the app or service you choose.

Children

The app is a planning tool for adults and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/bojansurbatovic-web/technical-officials-scheduler/issues.

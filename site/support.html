Technical Officials Scheduler

Getting started

  1. Open Events and tap New event, or open one of the examples.
  2. In Setup, add your races (date, start time, format, category) and your officials.
  3. Open Plan → Assignments. The planner fills every position; choose a person in any row to fix it.
  4. Check Conflicts, then export from More → Export & share.

Questions and problems

Please open an issue at github.com/bojansurbatovic-web/technical-officials-scheduler/issues and describe what you did, what you expected and your device.

Moving events between devices

Export the Plan file (.wtplan.json) and open it on the other device – it also opens in the Ubuntu desktop version.

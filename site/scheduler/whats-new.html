Technical Officials Scheduler/What’s new

What’s new

Technical Officials Scheduler

What changed in each version. The apps update through the App Store, the Microsoft Store and the Snap Store; the web app is always the latest version.

Version 1.0.4 (iPhone, iPad, Mac, Windows, web) — November 2026

  • Race panels stay open. The Course, Paces and Daylight and tides panels on the Races page no longer close after every entry – they open and close only when you click their triangle.

Version 1.0.3 (iPhone, iPad, Windows, web; on the Mac from 1.0.4) · Ubuntu 1.5.0 — November 2026

  • Sunrise and sunset for every race. Enter the venue’s coordinates and time zone on the Event page and the app calculates sunrise and sunset for each race day – offline. You can also type them in by hand.
  • Races stay in daylight. If a race would start before sunrise, or its last finisher would come in after sunset, the app moves the start and every duty, meeting and the athletes’ guide follow. The margins (15 minutes by default) can be changed per race, and the check can be switched off.
  • Tides on race day. Add high and low water with heights for each race. The app shows the tide at the start (for example “rising tide at the start: high water 08:40, 1 h 55 min after the start”) and water notes for athletes – on the Races page, in the athletes’ guide and in the Excel export.
  • TO certification level. Every official can have a level – 1, 2, 3a, 3b or 3c – in the officials list, when pasting names and in the Excel export.
  • What’s new in the app. A What’s new page under More (Ubuntu: menu › What’s new), shown once after an update.

Version 1.0.2 (iPhone, iPad, Mac, web) — November 2026

  • Opening animation. Swim, bike and run play for a moment while the app starts, with the app’s name and www.trisport.me. Tap to skip.
  • TriSport.me page in the app, with the website, help and our other apps.

Windows — November 2026

  • Technical Officials Scheduler for Windows 10 and 11, in the Microsoft Store or as an installer.

Version 1.0.1 · Ubuntu 1.4 — November 2026

  • Athletes’ guide. The briefing, race-day times, course and key rules for athletes only – share it as a page or a calendar.
  • Ubuntu: available from the Snap Store, with a fix so it starts reliably.

Version 1.0.0 — November 2026

  • First release: positions, short jobs, meetings and briefings from the World Triathlon rules, Gantt chart, personal schedules, timeline, Excel and calendar export.

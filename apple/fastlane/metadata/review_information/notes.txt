2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Technical Officials Scheduler plans the Technical Officials (referees) of triathlon races. Its users are race organisers, technical delegates and chief officials of triathlon clubs, national federations and event organisers anywhere in the world – any organiser that runs races under the World Triathlon Competition Rules, which national federations also use. Planning who works where is normally done by hand in spreadsheets: each official needs a main position, short jobs must not overlap in time, and the rules require briefings and meetings at fixed times. The app does this automatically: from the races (format, date, start time) and the list of officials it gives every official a position, adds short jobs without time clashes, books the required meetings (athletes' briefing, Jury meetings, rehearsals), counts radios, and warns about conflicts. The result can be viewed as a list, personal schedules, a Gantt chart and a timeline, and exported to Excel, calendar files or pictures.

3. HOW TO USE IT (no login, no sample files needed)
- On first launch an example event opens (two Elite races, 18 placeholder officials "TO 01"–"TO 18").
- Plan tab → Assignments: the generated plan. Choose another official in any row; the plan updates and the row is locked.
- Setup tab → Races: change a start time or add a race; Officials: rename an official or change the number of officials; Meetings & briefings: the rule-based meetings.
- Views tab → Gantt / Personal schedules / Timeline.
- More tab → Export & share: Excel, LibreOffice, calendar (.ics), Gantt picture or plan file through the standard share sheet.
- Events tab → New event: creates your own event; events are saved automatically on the device.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
None. The app makes no network requests and uses no data providers, authentication, payment, analytics, advertising or AI services. All planning runs on the device. The only outside interaction is the iOS share sheet when the user exports a file.
